# Visual asset boundary

The MIT license for the website code does not grant reuse rights to the portfolio covers, the photographs or brands depicted inside them, or the computer point-cloud geometry. These assets are retained for this portfolio's presentation. Rights remain with their respective rights holders; do not claim or redistribute them as your own portfolio material.

To reuse the template, replace dist/media/ with your own authorized artwork and update dist/content/projects.json and the selected-work images in dist/index.html. The smaller cover derivatives use the suffix -small.webp. Recheck the visible picture as well as its metadata.

The transition device geometry has separate notices in THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md. Fonts retain their SIL Open Font License files in LICENSES/.

The 2026-10-18 selected covers for ?, Reprise and Mac Setup were edited with the built-in ImageGen tool from their existing cover/interface references. Their new versioned WebP files and 768px derivatives are under dist/media/. Original images and prior versions are retained. Prompts, generated PNG originals, source references and browser QA are kept outside the public source package. Mac Setup's purple references Apple's published default light system palette: https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/color .
